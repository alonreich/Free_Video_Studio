# Free Video Studio — What's New & Release Changelog

What people who use Free Video Studio read: plain-English notes about what changed for them. The latest 5 releases are kept here; older ones move to [`CHANGELOG_ARCHIVE.md`](CHANGELOG_ARCHIVE.md) and are never deleted. Every version heading here is a published release (RELRECORD_01).

> **How this file works (RELNOTES_01 — full rules in [`09_DISTRIBUTION_AND_RELEASE.md`](09_DISTRIBUTION_AND_RELEASE.md#DIST-RELEASENOTES)):**
> new notes go under `## Upcoming` — never under a guessed version number. One bullet per user-visible change, `**Headline:** one or two plain sentences`; merge with an existing bullet instead of adding a near-duplicate; at most 15 bullets, each under 320 characters; no backticks, code, file names or engineering tags.
> When an official release is published, `## Upcoming` becomes that release's GitHub notes and the "What's new" on the in-app update card, is renamed to `## v<version> — <date>`, and a fresh `## Upcoming` appears. Technical details belong in [`changes.md`](changes.md).

## Upcoming
- **AI Commentary, Upgraded:** Pick or mix caption styles, see live progress and cost, and right-click lines to rewrite them. Long videos caption about twice as fast. Captions avoid HUD pieces and memes, match the saved video, and long ones stay in the picture. Highlights work in any language. Wrong times are flagged.
- **AI Online or On Your Own PC:** One plain question: on this PC (free), online, or both. Smart Zoom follows your target on your PC, on the processor if the graphics card fails. A recent result is offered free before you pay again. If the AI declines a clip it says so. Works with Google, Anthropic and OpenAI.
- **One Simple AI Setup:** Three easy steps: open the key page, come back, connected. A broken key, no credit or a used-up free day opens the right fix and says nothing was charged. Your key is checked at start; Settings shows one AI status line. The start screen no longer shows AI notices.
- **AI Kill Tracker:** Marks your kills with a red ✕, fast: your PC finds the likely moments and the AI only confirms them. Cheap scans start by themselves; dearer ones wait for your click, in the Video Merger too. Failures say why. Switch it on or off on the start screen.
- **Viral Post Helper:** When your export finishes, the AI shows live progress steps, elapsed time, and token estimates, reuses video frames across new versions so regenerations are fast, lets you edit the title and caption before copying, and says honestly when something fails.
- **AI Music Auto-Fit:** Shows the cost first, live steps and seconds, and can be cancelled. Your PC listens to your songs first (energy, beat), so the AI picks by sound, not just by name. Works with Merger timelines, and Ctrl+Z puts your songs back.
- **Simpler Add Music:** Two steps, multiple songs with resizable blocks, quiet intros and endings trimmed automatically, and a clear coverage bar. Music no longer ends early because the video speed changed; it keeps its short closing fade. Removing music from the timeline can be undone.
- **What You Hear Is What You Get:** Every screen plays your music, voice and memes together, like the saved video. Speed changes no longer make music or voice stutter. Voice slider goes to 150%. Voice Over opens twice as fast. The Speed Editor no longer locks up the first time after an update.
- **Video Merger Upgrades:** Drag clip blocks to reorder videos. AI captions stay with the merge and burn into exports. Preview flows across joined clips, and selected clips move or delete together. A block across two clips splits at the clip edge in one click for AI Smart Zoom and AI Commentary.
- **Timeline Markers & Music Credits:** Setting Mark End pauses preview at the chosen point, and clicking Play continues past the mark to inspect cut footage. Dragging markers scrubs the playhead smoothly without getting stuck when focus changes. Song credits open easily from export cards.
- **A Much Shorter Settings Window:** One simple choice per idea: updates, click sounds, how new videos start, one save folder and file name, one AI company. Light theme is readable (timeline clocks too), and errors say in plain words what to do and stay up long enough to read.
- **Know What's New, Before and After You Update:** The update card lists what the new version brings, the first start after updating shows it again, and Settings › About opens the full notes. A ready update installs when you close the app and Pause stays paused. An update that can't download by itself says so.
- **Open Source, Safer Updates, No Lost Work:** Free software (GPL v3), no tracking. Updates check certificates and roll back on faults. You are asked before edits or a running export would be lost; a finished video closes without questions. After a crash you get back your place, kill marks and sound choices.
- **Movie-Style Freeze Frames:** Freezes ring the Church Bell, which fades as the picture moves on, or pick Cinematic Dramatic Slow-Down in Settings › Sound & Music (press Play to hear it). It plays just below your video's own volume, the same on every screen and in the export. Your last freeze length is remembered.
- **Memes, Rebuilt:** Memes stay where you put them, match the final video, and play on the main screen too. The picker previews, trims, positions and styles memes; corner memes drag and lower game sound; kept music starts and stops on time. Starter memes and songs are now original or Creative-Commons, credited.
