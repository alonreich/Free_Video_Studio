# Free Video Studio — What's New & Release Changelog

What people who use Free Video Studio read: plain-English notes about what changed for them. The latest 5 releases are kept here; older ones move to [`CHANGELOG_ARCHIVE.md`](CHANGELOG_ARCHIVE.md) and are never deleted. Every version heading here is a published release (RELRECORD_01).

> **How this file works (RELNOTES_01 — full rules in [`09_DISTRIBUTION_AND_RELEASE.md`](09_DISTRIBUTION_AND_RELEASE.md#DIST-RELEASENOTES)):**
> new notes go under `## Upcoming` — never under a guessed version number. One bullet per user-visible change, `**Headline:** one or two plain sentences`; merge with an existing bullet instead of adding a near-duplicate; at most 15 bullets; no code, file names or engineering tags.
> When an official release is published, `## Upcoming` becomes that release's GitHub notes and the "What's new" on the in-app update card, is renamed to `## v<version> — <date>`, and a fresh `## Upcoming` appears. Technical details belong in [`changes.md`](changes.md).

## Upcoming
- **AI Commentary, Upgraded:** Pick a style or mix Funny, Sarcastic, Energetic and Tactical yourself. Captions accurately align across merged video clips, speak Hebrew, land on the action, a right-click rewrites one line, and clear messages explain when lines span between clips.
- **AI Works With Google, Anthropic and OpenAI, Everywhere:** Every AI feature works with whichever company you choose, uses their current models, and switches to a working model by itself if one is retired.
- **One Simple AI Setup:** Paste any AI key into one box and the app recognises the company by itself. Saving the key is your permission, so AI features no longer stop to ask again. The Crop Tool has its own AI button.
- **See What Each AI Job Used:** After AI Smart Zoom or AI Music, a clear notice displays the tokens used and remaining allowance for that session.
- **AI Kill Tracker:** When turned on, the app marks each of your kills with a red ✕ on the timeline and in the speed editor. Click a ✕ to jump straight to it, or Cancel while it scans.
- **Viral Post Helper:** When your export finishes, the AI writes a title, caption and hashtags. One button copies everything for YouTube or TikTok, writing can be cancelled, and it says honestly when it cannot.
- **AI Music Auto-Fit:** The Add Music wizard can pick the best song for your video's mood, shows each step it takes, can be cancelled, and one Ctrl+Z brings your old songs back.
- **Simpler Add Music:** Two steps, multiple songs with resizable blocks, quiet intros and endings trimmed automatically, and a clear coverage bar. Music no longer ends early because the video speed changed; it keeps its short closing fade.
- **What You Hear Is What You Get:** Voice recordings no longer lose volume when saved to video. A new voice slider goes from silent to 150%, snaps to normal at 100%, and carries your choice through previews and saved videos. Song previews stop when you move on and no longer freeze while scrubbing.
- **Video Merger Upgrades:** Drag clip blocks on the timeline to reorder videos with instant queue list updates. AI commentary captions stay saved with the merge and burn into exported videos. Preview playback seamlessly continues across joined clips, and multi-selected clips move or delete together.
- **Timeline Markers & Music Credits:** Setting Mark End pauses preview at the chosen point, and clicking Play continues past the mark to inspect cut footage. Dragging markers scrubs the playhead smoothly without getting stuck when focus changes. Song credits open easily from export cards.
- **A Clearer Settings Window:** 6 simple tabs, a search bar, plain help, Backup/Import and Restore buttons. It asks before discarding changes, and choices like speed now work. The app has a new icon for taskbar and tray. Project saving and opening stay responsive; failed saves keep your edits open.
- **Know What's New, Before and After You Update:** The update card lists what the new version brings, the first start after updating shows it again, and Settings › About opens the full notes. A ready update installs when you close the app, Pause stays paused, and the Beta choice really gets test versions first.
- **Smaller, Safer Updates:** Updates download changes only, check certificates and roll back on faults. Other accounts on a shared PC can no longer listen in on the app's private channels. Development shares music and memes, older data is kept, and uninstall refuses linked or locked folders.
- **Memes, Rebuilt:** Memes stay where you put them, match the final video, and play on the main screen too. The picker previews, trims, positions and styles memes; corner memes drag and lower game sound; kept music starts and stops on time. Starter and downloadable memes and songs are now original or royalty-free.
